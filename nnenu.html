Nnenu

A field guide for temporary spaces.

Nnenu helps people discover specific things inside exhibitions, markets and complex indoor events, then record what is missing with others.

  • Product design
  • Mobile UX
  • Spatial experience

01

Project snapshot

The context, brief and current state in four short moves.

Situation

  • Maps stop at venues; users still cannot see what is worth finding inside.
  • Official directories, social posts and screenshots fragment rapidly changing information.

Task

  • Organize a temporary space from area to shop to item.
  • Make discovery and contribution work as one connected experience.

Action

  • Designed progressive Explore and Create journeys.
  • Built reusable search, navigation, card and media patterns.
  • Identified content-first and camera-first priorities for the next iteration.

Current state

  • Core exploration, detail, authentication and creation flows are prototyped.
  • The next phase will validate discovery speed, creation effort and information freshness.

02

Two connected paths help people understand a place and contribute what is missing.

02.1

Explore from place to item

The journey moves from an area to a shop and then an item, preserving location context at every step.

02.2

Create with one reusable pattern

Menus, shops and food share the same logic. The shop flow shows the full sequence from choosing a type and location to images, labels and preview.
